# SpiritFeed

A tiny, minimalist, password-gated social app for a friend group of ~15-20 people. Share a photo, share a status, see how everyone's doing, and let your spirit animal represent you.

This is not trying to be Instagram. No algorithm, no public discovery, no ads, no strangers. It's a private clubhouse for one specific friend group, built to be a little weird and a little fun.

## What it does

- Post a photo with an optional short caption to a simple chronological feed
- Set a short status line (like an old AIM/MSN away message)
- React to posts with emoji — no comment threads, keeps things low-pressure
- Every user picks a **spirit animal** on signup, which becomes their permanent avatar across the whole app
- A live **mood dashboard** showing everyone's current status/emoji at a glance
- **Streaks** and a posting heatmap, tracking who shows up
- **Time capsule** posts that stay locked until a future date you choose
- An auto-generated **weekly recap** digest every Sunday
- Invite-link onboarding — no public signup, ever
